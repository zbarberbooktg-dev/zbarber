---
name: Premium launch state
description: Product rule for keeping current features free while preserving future Premium infrastructure.
---

All currently available Zbarber features must remain free. Subscription, financing, conference, and other genuinely paid capabilities must be labeled “À venir” / “Coming soon” and must not expose working activation, assignment, submission, or processing actions. Keep their backend models and routes intact for future development. Marketing phrases such as “premium grooming” are positioning language and should not be treated as paid-feature labels.

**Why:** Premium has not launched yet, and the product must not imply that users can buy or request unavailable services while still preserving the technical foundation for a later release.

**How to apply:** When touching mobile, admin, or vitrine Premium surfaces, keep free booking and salon-management flows enabled, mark paid capabilities as coming soon in both languages, and leave future subscription infrastructure recoverable.
